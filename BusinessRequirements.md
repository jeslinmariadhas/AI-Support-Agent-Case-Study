Business Requirements Document
Project

24/7 AI Support Agent

Customer
CloudDesk (Fictional SaaS Company)

Background
ClouDesk is a SaaS company providing managing software over to 15000 customers. 

The customer support team currently handles aproximately 600 tickets per day through website chat and emails.

The support team has 12 agents and approximately 75% of incoming tickets are repitative leading to konger response times and reduced customer statisfaction.

Current Challenges
   Long response times
   High support workload
   Repetitive tickets
   Limited support outside business hours
   Manual ticket routing
   Slow escalation process

Project Objective
Design and implement an AI-powered Support Agent capable of:
  Providing 24/7 customer support
  Answering FAQ questions
  Using company documentation
  Escalating complex issues
  Creating support tickets
  Summarizing conversations
  Reducing manual workload
